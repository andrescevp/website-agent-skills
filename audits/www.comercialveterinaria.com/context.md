# Audit Context: www.comercialveterinaria.com

## 1. Business Overview & Value Proposition
- **Brand:** Comercial Veterinaria
- **Core Activity:** Specialized e-commerce platform and physical pet retail store based in Burgos, Spain (running on PrestaShop 8 / PHP 8.3).
- **Product Scope:** Comprehensive veterinary and pet supplies: aquatics & fishkeeping (`acuariofilia`), dogs (`perros`), veterinary diets, supplements, food, hygiene, and accessories.

## 2. Target Audience
- Pet owners, aquarium hobbyists, and veterinary caretakers in Spain seeking specialized products, specialized nutrition, and technical aquarium equipment.
- Local customers in Burgos seeking physical store collection or local delivery.

## 3. Business Goals
- Primary Goal: E-commerce online sales conversions across product categories (food, health, aquarium gear).
- Secondary Goal: Organic search rankings for high-intent pet/veterinary queries and local search visibility in Burgos.

## 4. Key Discovery Findings
- The catalog is structured with rich category taxonomy but currently lacks modern Schema.org markup (no `Product`, `Store`, or `BreadcrumbList` in JSON-LD) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) context (`llms.txt`).

# Audit Context: mentoria24.com

## 1. Business Overview & Value Proposition
- **Brand:** Mentoria 24
- **Core Offering:** Intensive 1:1 AI mentoring program consisting of 24 practical hours with senior software and data engineers.
- **Value Proposition:** Empowers professionals and business owners to reduce repetitive workload by 50% and achieve practical autonomy using AI tools without long subscriptions.

## 2. Target Audience
- Non-technical and semi-technical professionals, entrepreneurs, managers, and SMB owners seeking rapid productivity gains.
- Geographic focus: Spain and broader Spanish-speaking markets.

## 3. Key Conversion Goals
- Primary Goal: Schedule a free 1:1 strategic diagnosis session (`#cta`).
- Secondary Goal: Information transparency on curriculum, senior team credentials, and FAQ clarity.

## 4. Key Competitors & Differentiators
- Differentiator: Bounded 24-hour hands-on 1:1 engineering guidance vs. passive video courses or ongoing retainer subscriptions.
